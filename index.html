What we do

We design, build and look after gardens that suit the way you live.

Garden Dreams began with a folding table at Riverside Market and one stubborn idea: a garden should be planned for the ground it sits on, planted for the climate it lives in, and built by people who will still be there in ten years.

Today that is a four-person studio on Alder Lane, twelve partner nurseries within ninety miles, and a crew that carries a drawing through to the last plant going in.

  • Plants grown close to home — twelve partner nurseries within 90 miles.
  • One crew, start to finish — the people who draw it are the people who build it.
  • Priced after the survey — never from a distance, never from a template.

How we work

From first walk-through to the season after

Four stages, written down before we start. You always know what is happening, what it costs and what comes next.

01

Survey & brief

We measure the plot, read the light, note the soil and listen to how you want to use the space. Nothing is quoted before this visit.

02

Design

You receive one drawing to react to — a scaled layout, a planting schedule and a materials list, with the cultivars named rather than suggested.

03

Build & plant

Our own crew handles groundworks, structures and planting on a written schedule, with materials coming straight from our partner nurseries.

04

Care & aftercare

We return through the settling period, replace anything that has not taken, and stay on a seasonal care plan if you want the garden maintained.

In season

What is doing the work right now

A garden is a set of seasons rather than a single photograph. Here is what we are planting, pruning and cutting back in each of them.

Spring

Clear, cut and feed

Cut back the late perennials, work compost into the beds, plant the first bulbs and divide anything that has crowded itself out.

Summer

Tidy and irrigate

Deadheading, weeding and irrigation checks. The garden is at its fullest here, and the work is about keeping it that way without wasting water.

Autumn

Plant for spring

The heaviest planting window: perennials, shrubs, bulbs and ground cover go in now while the soil is still warm and the rain does the watering.

Winter

Prune and plan

Structural pruning while the shape is visible, leaf clearance, and a written plan for what changes in the year ahead.

Our commitments

Four things we do not bend

These are the rules we work by, written down so you can hold us to them.

01

Grown close to home

Twelve partner nurseries within 90 miles of the studio. Shorter journeys mean plants that arrive in the ground the same week they leave the pot.

02

Designed for the site

No two gardens get the same drawing. Light, slope, soil and the shape of your day decide the planting, not the other way round.

03

One crew, end to end

The designer who draws the plan is on site while it is built. Nothing is lost in translation between the drawing and the ground.

04

Priced honestly

Every garden is quoted after a survey, in writing, with quantities named. No templates, no padded allowances, no surprises on the invoice.
