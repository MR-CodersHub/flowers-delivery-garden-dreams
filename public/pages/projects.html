What a project record contains

Every job is written down at the same four moments, so a client always knows what has been decided, what is happening on site and what it will cost.

  • Survey notes — plot, light, soil, access and drainage.
  • A scaled drawing — layout, materials and named cultivars.
  • A build schedule — groundworks, planting windows and sequencing.
  • An aftercare list — what is planted, watered and replaced.
