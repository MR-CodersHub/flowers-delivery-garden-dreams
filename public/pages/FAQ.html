Orders & delivery

Placing, tracking and receiving your flowers.

Same-day delivery is available for orders placed before 2pm, seven days a week. Subscriptions follow your chosen window, and priority slots (8–10am or 5–7pm) can be added at checkout.
We call the recipient first, then leave the bouquet with a neighbour or in a safe, shaded spot — always in water with a note. You receive a photo either way.
Your dashboard shows the live status of every order, and we send a message the moment the courier leaves the studio with an estimated arrival window.
The city ring road is free, up to 10 miles is $6 and up to 25 miles is $12. Beyond that, send us a message — we quote long-distance runs individually.

Freshness, ingredients and keeping blooms alive.

Seven to ten days with basic care: trim the stems at an angle every two days, change the water, keep them out of direct sun and away from fruit bowls. If yours fade within a week, we remake it free.

Subscriptions & billing

Plans, skipping, upgrades and cancelling.

Any time, from your dashboard, before Sunday of that week. Skipping is free and unlimited — billing simply pauses and resumes on its own.
Two clicks in your dashboard, no phone call and no fee. Your final scheduled delivery still arrives as normal, and you can reactivate whenever you like.
Two months free on every plan — roughly 17% — charged once a year instead of monthly. You can switch back to monthly at your next renewal.
Corporate accounts receive one consolidated monthly invoice with a statement per delivery location. Email us and we will set up terms within a day.
