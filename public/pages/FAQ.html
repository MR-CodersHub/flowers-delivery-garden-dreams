Surveys & quotes

How we start, and what a price is based on.

We measure the plot, note the light, look at the soil and drainage, check access, and talk through how you want to use the space. It usually takes 45 to 90 minutes. Nothing is priced before this visit, and there is no charge for it.
From the work and the materials, listed separately so you can see what you are paying for. We do not publish a standard price list because no two gardens are the same, and we do not add allowances for work that may not be needed.
Yes. A replanted border or a single path is a legitimate project and gets the same care as a full build. If a job is not a good fit for us, we will say so at the survey rather than after the invoice.
Most full builds take six to ten weeks from survey to completion, with the plant ordering scheduled against the planting window. Smaller planting-only jobs can often be done within two to three weeks. We will tell you the real timescale at the survey.

Design & planting

Drawings, soil, plant choice and what happens after planting.

Both. A scaled layout showing the beds, paths, levels and structures, plus a planting schedule with every plant named by cultivar and quantity, and a materials list. One revision round is included as standard.
Because it decides everything. You can buy any plant you like, but if the ground underneath is compacted, poor or draining badly, the plant will struggle whatever we do. Soil improvement is quoted separately so you can see it, and it is usually the best value in the whole project.
From twelve partner nurseries within 90 miles of the studio. Shorter journeys mean plants reach the ground in the same week they leave the pot, and we buy directly from the growers rather than through a middleman.
Yes — tell us the species, the colour or the plant you have in mind at the survey. We will use them where the site will support them, and where it will not we will say so and propose an alternative that will. Naming a plant that suits the soil is worth more than naming one you have seen elsewhere.

Build, hardscape & water

Groundworks, materials, drainage and irrigation.

Our own crew. We do not subcontract the groundworks or the planting to a third party — the person who drew the plan is on site while it is built. Where specialist trades are genuinely needed, we tell you who they are and why.
For how they will look in ten years, not ten weeks. Stone, brick and gravel all behave differently depending on your soil and your climate, and we will show you samples and explain the trade-offs — including where a cheaper option will need replacing sooner.
Often, yes. We start with a runoff survey and level survey, then look at what is practical: re-profiling the ground, a planted swale or basin, or a rain garden that holds water on site instead of pushing it to the boundary. Where the answer is above ground, we will say so rather than promising a below-ground fix.
Yes. We design, install and hand over the system with a plain-language guide so it works without us coming back. Dripline for beds and borders, proper zoning, and a timer that suits the planting rather than a fixed half hour every day.

Ongoing care

Seasonal visits, planting windows and what is guaranteed.

We come back through the settling period to check what has taken, replace anything that has not, and hand over a written aftercare list. If you would rather we kept going, our seasonal care plans continue that work across the year.
Four times a year on a full care plan — one round per season, timed to the work that season needs. Each visit is followed by a short written report covering what was done, what was noticed and what we would recommend next.
Anything that does not take in its first season is replaced on us. We will also tell you honestly if a failure was the plant's fault, the soil's fault or the site's — and what we would change before we plant it again.
Yes, at any time and with no fee — travel, a house move, or simply a season where the garden does not need us. Pause from your dashboard and the schedule picks up again when you are ready.
