1. The agreement

By accepting a proposal for design, build work or ongoing care from Garden Dreams Studio, you agree to these terms. If you are commissioning on behalf of a company, you confirm you are authorised to bind it.

"We" means Garden Dreams Studio of 14 Alder Lane, Riverside District. "You" means the property owner or the organisation you represent.

2. Proposals & acceptance

A proposal is our offer to carry out a defined scope. A contract forms when you accept it in writing and we confirm — not when a survey is booked. We may withdraw or amend a proposal before acceptance if:

  • A survey reveals conditions that change the scope — soil, drainage, access or buried services.
  • Site access cannot be granted on the dates the work requires.
  • A pricing or typographical error is obvious on the document.
  • We are unable to secure the specified materials within the stated window.

Where the site changes the scope, we tell you what we found, what it costs and what we recommend, and you decide before any work starts.

3. Prices & payment

All prices include applicable sales tax unless stated otherwise. Every proposal separates labour, materials, plant supply and any specialist work so you can see what you are paying for.

  • Build projects are staged against agreed milestones, typically a deposit at acceptance and the balance on completion.
  • Care plans are billed per visit or as a scheduled plan for the season ahead.
  • Plant quantities may vary within the agreed schedule; we do not buy against a fixed list and charge you for what is planted.
  • Travel and any accommodation for crews working away from the studio are quoted separately.

If a payment fails we will try again after three days and notify you before any further attempt.

4. Site access & scheduling

Most gardens are worked on in a single coordinated visit or a short programme of visits. Dates are agreed before work begins, and we will tell you as early as possible if weather, ground conditions or supply hold-ups force a change.

  • You are responsible for providing access to the working area, and for telling us about buried services, septic tanks and existing irrigation.
  • Access routes must be safe and clear; if a route cannot be used safely we will stop and agree an alternative with you.
  • Work pauses when ground is saturated or frozen, because landscaping conditions matter more than a scheduled slot.
  • Finished areas are left clean and level on the day, with arisings removed unless you ask us to leave them for mulch.

5. Care plans

Care plans renew by agreement and can be paused, changed or ended at any time from your dashboard.

  • Visits are scheduled to the season, so work happens when the garden needs it rather than on a fixed date.
  • Pause at no cost while you are away, and the schedule resumes when you are ready.
  • Plan changes take effect from your next scheduled visit; we adjust the price pro rata.
  • We may change plan pricing with 30 days' written notice; the new price applies from your next cycle.

Every care visit is followed by a short written report covering what was done, what was noticed and what we would recommend next.

6. Planting & guarantees

Plants are living material and we take responsibility for how they are selected, sourced and planted:

  • We guarantee that plants healthy at the time of planting, with roots prepared and holes dug correctly.
  • Anything that does not establish in its first season is replaced on us, provided the aftercare list was followed.
  • Losses caused by drought, frost, animals, neglect or works carried out by others are not covered.
  • Natural variation is expected — colour, habit and exact mature size may differ from photographs and drawings.

7. Cancellations & refunds

  • Before work starts — cancel at no cost. We charge only for work already carried out and materials already ordered on your instruction.
  • Once work is underway — we charge for the part of the work completed to that point, and for any plant or material already bought in for your project.
  • Care plans — cancel any time with no fee; visits not yet carried out are not charged.
  • Weather and access — where work cannot proceed because of conditions outside our control, we reschedule at no extra cost.

Statutory rights are unaffected by anything written here.

8. Liability

We supply design and landscaping services with reasonable skill and care. Living material varies: colour, habit and exact mature size may differ from photographs, and images are indicative only.

  • We are not liable for indirect or consequential loss, including loss of enjoyment.
  • Our total liability for any project is limited to the amount you paid for the work concerned.
  • Nothing in these terms limits liability for death, personal injury or fraud where such limitation is unlawful.
  • Tell us about any issue within 14 days of completion so we have a fair chance to put it right.

9. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the country in which the studio operates, and its courts have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute that we cannot resolve directly.
