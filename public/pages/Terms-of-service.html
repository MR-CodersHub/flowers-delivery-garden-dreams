1. The agreement

By placing an order, starting a subscription or confirming an event booking with Garden Dreams Studio, you agree to these terms. If you are booking on behalf of a company, you confirm you are authorised to bind it.

"We" means Garden Dreams Studio of 14 Alder Lane, Riverside District. "You" means the person placing the order or the organisation they represent.

2. Ordering & acceptance

An order is an offer to buy. A contract forms when we send your order confirmation — not when you click pay. We may decline an order before confirmation if:

  • The requested blooms are unavailable and no acceptable substitute exists.
  • The delivery address falls outside our coverage and no arrangement has been made.
  • A pricing or typographical error is obvious on the page.
  • We suspect the transaction is fraudulent.

Where we must substitute a stem, we always keep the colour palette, style and value of your order at least equal to what you paid.

3. Prices & payment

All prices include applicable sales tax unless stated otherwise. Delivery charges are shown before you pay and depend on distance from the studio.

  • One-off orders are charged in full at checkout.
  • Monthly subscriptions are charged on the first of each month for the month ahead.
  • Annual subscriptions are charged once, and include two months free.
  • Event bookings require a 30% deposit, with the balance due 14 days before the event.

If a payment fails we will try again after three days and notify you before any further attempt.

4. Delivery

We deliver seven days a week. Same-day service applies to orders confirmed before 2pm local time. Delivery windows are estimates, not guarantees, unless you purchased a priority slot.

  • You are responsible for providing accurate recipient details and access instructions.
  • If nobody is home we may leave the bouquet with a neighbour or in a safe, shaded place, always in water, and notify you with a photo.
  • Failed deliveries caused by incorrect addresses may incur a redelivery fee.
  • Risk passes to you once the bouquet is delivered to the recipient, a neighbour or the nominated safe place.

5. Subscriptions

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. You can skip, pause, change plan or cancel from your dashboard at any time.

  • Skipping is free and unlimited when done before Sunday of the delivery week.
  • Plan changes take effect from the next billing cycle; we prorate any difference.
  • Cancellation takes effect immediately for future billing; your final scheduled delivery still arrives.
  • We may change plan pricing with 30 days' written notice; the new price applies from your next cycle.

The freshness promise applies to every subscription delivery: seven days of vase life or we remake it free.

6. Weddings & events

Event bookings become binding once the deposit is received and the proposal is countersigned. Key conditions:

  • Final guest numbers, scope and venue details are confirmed 14 days before the event.
  • Floral quantities may vary by up to 10% in either direction, priced pro rata, because flowers are a crop, not a component.
  • Venue access, power and parking arrangements are your responsibility unless agreed otherwise.
  • Repurposing plans described in the proposal are included at no extra charge.

7. Cancellations & refunds

  • One-off orders — cancel free of charge before dispatch. After dispatch, contact us within 24 hours if the flowers arrive damaged and we will remake and redeliver, or refund in full.
  • Subscriptions — cancel any time with no fee; undelivered future deliveries are refunded automatically.
  • Events — deposits are refundable up to 60 days before the event, and non-refundable inside 30 days because grower orders and crew are already committed.
  • Workshops — transferable to another person up to 48 hours before the session; non-refundable inside 48 hours.

Statutory rights are unaffected by anything written here.

8. Liability

We supply flowers with reasonable skill and care. Natural products vary: colour, stem count and exact bloom shape may differ from photographs, and images are indicative only.

  • We are not liable for indirect or consequential loss, including loss of enjoyment.
  • Our total liability for any order is limited to the amount you paid for it.
  • Nothing in these terms limits liability for death, personal injury or fraud where such limitation is unlawful.
  • Notify us of any issue within 24 hours of delivery so we have a fair chance to put it right.

9. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the country in which the studio operates, and its courts have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute that we cannot resolve directly.

We would always rather talk it through first — email hello@gardendreams.studio and a manager will respond within one business day.
