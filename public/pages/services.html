How every project works

The same four stages, whatever the size

A single replanted bed and a full landscape build follow the same route, so you always know where the work has reached.

01

Survey the site

We measure the plot, read the light, test the soil, look at the drainage and talk through how you use the space. Nothing is priced before this visit.

02

Draw it up

You get one scaled layout with the planting schedule and the material list, with cultivars named rather than suggested. One drawing, then one revision.

03

Build and plant

Groundworks, structures and planting run to a written schedule by our own crew, with material coming straight from our partner nurseries.

04

Settle and care

We return through the settling period, replace anything that has not taken, and hand over a seasonal care plan if you want the garden kept.

What every job carries with it

These are not extras. They are part of how we work on every site, from the smallest replant to the largest build.

Measured survey

Levels, access, boundaries and light, recorded on the day so the drawing and the quote match the ground.

Scaled drawing

One layout plan with the planting schedule and the material list, named in full before anything is ordered.

Written schedule

Groundworks, material deliveries, planting windows and the order they happen in — agreed before the first dig.

Named cultivars

Specific plants, not categories — chosen for your soil, your light and the size the space will hold in five years.

Irrigation handover

Zones, timers and a plain-language guide so the system works without us coming back.

Seasonal care plan

A year-round programme of visits and cut-backs, with a short written report after each round.
