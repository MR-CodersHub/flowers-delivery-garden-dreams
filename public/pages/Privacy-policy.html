1. Who we are

Garden Dreams Studio ("we", "us", "our") operates the flower subscription, delivery and event floristry service found at this website. For the purposes of data protection law we are the controller of your personal information.

Studio address: 14 Alder Lane, Riverside District. Email: privacy@gardendreams.studio.

2. What we collect

We collect information in three ways: what you give us, what happens when you use the site, and what our delivery partners tell us.

  • Account details — name, email address, phone number and password (stored only as a hash).
  • Delivery details — recipient names, addresses, safe-place instructions and gift messages.
  • Payment details — handled entirely by our payment processor; we never see or store full card numbers.
  • Usage data — pages viewed, referring pages, device type and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Preferences — flowers you dislike, delivery windows, billing period and any notes you add to your profile.

3. How we use it

Every use of your information has a legal basis. The table below covers the main ones.

  • Contract — to take payment, arrange deliveries, manage your subscription and respond to order issues.
  • Legitimate interest — to improve the site, prevent fraud and keep our service secure and reliable.
  • Consent — to send you the Morning Journal and occasional offers. You withdraw consent with one click in any email.
  • Legal obligation — to keep accurate financial records for tax purposes.

We do not use your information to build advertising profiles, and we never sell, rent or trade personal data with third parties.

4. Cookies & tracking

We use a small number of cookies and browser storage keys. None of them are used for cross-site advertising.

  • Strictly necessary — keeping you signed in, protecting forms from abuse and remembering your basket.
  • Preferences — your chosen theme (light or dark) and reading direction, stored locally on your device only.
  • Analytics — aggregated, IP-truncated page statistics that help us understand what people actually read.

You can clear or block cookies in your browser at any time; the site stays fully usable, though you will need to sign in again after each visit.

5. Who we share with

We share the minimum necessary, only with organisations that help us run the service:

  • Payment processor — to authorise transactions and detect fraud.
  • Delivery couriers — recipient name, address and delivery window for that order only.
  • Email provider — your address, if you subscribed to the journal, so we can send it.
  • Host & CDN — technical logs required to serve the site securely.

All of these partners are contractually bound to protect your data and may not use it for their own purposes.

6. How long we keep it

  • Order records — seven years, because tax law requires it.
  • Account profiles — until you close the account, then deleted within 30 days.
  • Marketing consent — until you unsubscribe; a suppression list is kept so we do not email you again by mistake.
  • Support messages — two years from the last contact.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can:

  • Ask for a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is inaccurate or out of date.
  • Ask us to delete your information, subject to legal retention duties.
  • Restrict or object to how we use it.
  • Receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw marketing consent at any time, without affecting your service.

We answer verified requests within 30 days and never charge for a first request.

8. Security

All traffic is encrypted with TLS, passwords are hashed with a modern algorithm, access to customer data inside the studio is limited to the florists who need it, and every staff account uses multi-factor authentication.

If we ever suffer a personal data breach that risks your rights, we will notify the relevant authority within 72 hours and tell you directly if the risk to you is high.

9. Contact us

Questions, complaints or requests go to our privacy lead at privacy@gardendreams.studio, or by post to 14 Alder Lane, Riverside District. If you are unhappy with our response you may raise the matter with your local data protection authority.
