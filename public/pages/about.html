OUR STORY

Grown close, cut at dawn,
arranged by hand

What started as a Saturday stall at the Riverside Market became a full studio, a weekly subscription, and a wedding team — without ever changing how we buy flowers.

We still know every grower by name. We still reject anything that travelled further than it had to. And we still hand-tie every bouquet on the same scarred oak bench we started with.

  • 12 partner farms within 90 miles of the studio.
  • Cut-to-order — nothing sits in a cold room for weeks.
  • Plastic-free wrapping on every single order.
WHAT DRIVES US

OUR MISSION & VISION

Two sentences we use to decide every yes and every no in the studio.

M

Mission

To make genuinely fresh, locally grown flowers an ordinary part of everyday life — not a luxury reserved for anniversaries — through honest subscriptions, seasonal bouquets and delivery that actually arrives on time.

V

Vision

A city where the flowers on your table can name the field they came from: a floristry industry that is seasonal by default, low-waste by design, and joyful without being wasteful.

01

Seasonal first

We design around what is blooming now, never around an imported photo.

02

Grower paid fairly

Direct relationships, published prices, paid on delivery day.

03

Zero-waste studio

Compost bins, reusable water packs, recycled cardboard only.

04

Joy, not gimmick

Beautiful flowers, plain pricing, no subscription traps.

THE JOURNEY

SEVEN YEARS, ONE DIRECTION

From a folding table to a full floral studio — the milestones that shaped Garden Dreams.

2019

The Saturday stall

Two crates, one bucket and a hand-painted sign at Riverside Market. We sold out by 10am every weekend for a year.

2021

The studio opens

A tiny glass-fronted workshop on Alder Lane with a proper cold room, a scarred oak bench and room for two florists.

2023

Weekly subscriptions launch

40 neighbours trusted us with their office desks and kitchen tables. 92% of them are still with us today.

2026

Weddings & corporate

A dedicated event crew, twelve partner farms and 96 weddings a year — while still hand-tying every bunch ourselves.
