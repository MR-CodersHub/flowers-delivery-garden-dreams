The studio

Four people, one crew, and a great deal of soil

Garden Dreams began as a folding table at Riverside Market in 2019. We are now a four-person studio on Alder Lane, designing, building and maintaining gardens across the Riverside District.

Our story

It started with plants we could not throw away

In 2019 we sold seasonal plants at Riverside Market because we could not keep up with what we were growing. The complaint was always the same: people wanted somebody to plant it properly.

So the work changed. We stopped simply supplying plants and started taking responsibility for the whole space — the soil, the layout, the hardscape, the planting scheme and what happens to it afterwards. Today the studio designs, builds and maintains gardens across the district, with twelve partner nurseries within ninety miles supplying the material.

The scale has not changed. We still work in whole gardens rather than departments, and the person who draws the plan is the person standing on site while it is built.

Mission & vision

What we are trying to build

Mission

To make a well-made garden the normal choice rather than the luxury one — by designing for the ground we are given, planting what the climate actually supports, and pricing honestly in writing.

Vision

A district where every garden is legible — where you can read what a designer intended, see what the site allowed, and walk into it and feel the difference between a planted space and a designed one.

What we hold to

Four working principles

01

Seasonal first

Planting is chosen for what the site can carry through the year, not for how it looks in one photograph on one afternoon.

02

Growers paid fairly

Our twelve partner nurseries are paid properly and bought from directly, so the quality of what reaches your garden holds up.

03

Low-waste practice

Green waste is chipped and returned to the soil on site wherever the space allows, and packaging is kept to a minimum.

04

Built to last

We specify materials that will still be standing in fifteen years, and we would rather reduce the scope than specify something that will not.

The road so far

How the studio grew

Four moments that changed what we do. Each one started with a client asking for something we could not yet deliver.

2019

The Saturday stall

Garden Dreams opens at Riverside Market with a folding table and seasonal plants grown by the founder. The first request we cannot fulfil is somebody asking us to plant what we sell.

2020

The studio on Alder Lane

We take on a small unit on Alder Lane as a base for the nursery, the drawing table and the store — and begin taking on whole sites rather than single beds.

2022

Design becomes the core of the job

Planting plans, levels and drainage drawings move to the front of every project. We start quoting from a survey rather than a standard price list.

2026

A full landscape service

Design, hardscape, planting, irrigation and seasonal maintenance are now handled end to end by the same four-person crew, with twelve partner nurseries supplying the material.
