The urban discipline

High brick walls, shifting shade, and zero room for error.

Designing a compact city courtyard is a fundamentally different craft than planting an acre of suburban lawn. In an urban home, the garden is an outdoor room lived in every single day — viewed constantly from behind kitchen bifolds and bedroom balconies.

If a specimen drops leaves or drainage clogs, there is nowhere to look away. We engineer each courtyard around reflection, acoustic buffering, clean transit logistics through domestic interiors, and botanical varieties that thrive in brick microclimates.

  • 3D Solar & Shadow Analysis — planting calculated for tall boundaries and canyon shade patterns.
  • Domestic Clean-Access Protocol — materials carried through living areas with zero dust or wall scuffs.
  • Silent Sub-Surface Driplines — automated irrigation with zero overspray on masonry or dining furniture.

Built to endure

The hidden engineering behind long-lived urban gardens

Small spaces demand higher technical precision. Here is how our studio solves the three most common failure points in town gardens.

Structural Load Engineering

For roof gardens and terraces, every kilogram counts. We formulate expanded volcanic clay soil blends and distribute loads precisely over structural joists.

Acoustic Buffer Design

City echoes amplify noise between party walls. We position dense broadleaf evergreens and bubbling basalt water features to mask sirens and traffic rumble.

Zoned Micro-Irrigation

Shadow cast by adjacent buildings means one border stays damp while another bakes dry. Our systems deliver targeted hydration per zone with zero surface run-off.
